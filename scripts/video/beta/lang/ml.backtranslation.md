# Malayalam narration — blind back-translation

Source files read (and nothing else): `scripts/video/beta/vo_script_v3.ml.json`, `scripts/video/beta/lang/ml.json`.
The English narration, drafts, review notes and builder scripts were NOT opened. The English in the "Literal English" column is what the Malayalam actually says, word for word where possible, not a polished re-write.

Legend for the opinion column: **natural** = a Kerala listener would not notice it was translated; **acceptable** = understood, but a native writer might phrase it differently; **stiff** = grammatical but bookish/awkward when spoken; **wrong** = says something different from what it seems to intend, or is ungrammatical. "[?]" marks a word I am not sure how a Kerala listener would take.

Mechanical check (run locally, both files): 12 sections in both files, sentence lists identical, all 42 anchor phrases appear verbatim inside their sentence. 36 screen-text entries.
